Fit Kid Hooper — Privacy Notice

Plain-language summary of what we collect and why. Last updated 2026-10-20.

Who this is for

Fit Kid Hooper is a basketball training app for young athletes (roughly ages 9–14). Anyone can train as a guest: guest progress stays on the device and nothing is saved to an account. To save progress and use friends, messages, and challenges, an athlete creates an account. For athletes under 13, a parent or guardian must give permission — we ask for the parent's email at sign-up and send them a link to approve. Athletes 13 and older can create an account themselves, using their own email.

What we collect

  • From the athlete: their first name, a Jersey Name (username) they choose, a 6-digit passcode, and the training info they enter (favorite players, position, goals, and birthday so we can set the right age group). Plus their in-app activity: practices, programs, shots logged, streaks, badges, and XP.
  • If the athlete adds one: a profile photo, and messages they send to friends.
  • From the parent/guardian: an email address, used to get permission and to keep the parent informed about their athlete's training.
  • Automatically: usage events (which screens are used and when) to improve the app. They are tied to the account or a random device ID — never to a child's contact information.

What we do with it

  • Save the athlete's progress so they never lose their streak, badges, or XP.
  • Show them on Challenges leaderboards by Jersey Name only — never their real name.
  • Let athletes find friends by name or invite code. Search results show the Jersey Name only; friends, and members of a squad challenge an athlete joins by invite, can see each other's profile, including first name and photo. Messages can only be sent between friends.
  • Email the parent about their athlete (for example, if they haven't trained in a while).
  • Send training reminders as phone notifications, only if the athlete turns them on.
  • Understand which features help kids train, so we can make the app better.

Coach FKH

Coach FKH answers training questions from our own drill library. To understand what a question is about, the text an athlete types may be sent to an AI service (OpenAI). We send only the question — not the athlete's name, account, or any other information.

Video training

Live video features are off unless a parent turns them on in Settings. Video calls are only between friends whose parents have both agreed, and in coach-led classes athletes join with their camera and microphone off.

Feedback

If an athlete sends feedback in the app, it goes to the Fit Kid Hooper team, including an alert on a team member's phone, so we can fix problems quickly.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use a child's information to target advertising, and we do not let anyone track your child across other apps and websites.
  • We do not collect an email address from athletes under 13 (we ask for a parent's instead), and we never collect precise location.

Videos

Some training videos play from YouTube inside the app. When one plays, YouTube receives the request and may show advertising and collect information under Google's own privacy policy. We do not send YouTube your child's name, account, or any information you have given us.

Who helps us run the app

We use a few service providers to run Fit Kid Hooper: Supabase (accounts and data storage), YouTube (training videos), OpenAI (understanding Coach FKH questions), and an email provider (Resend) for messages to parents. They handle data only to provide those services to us.

Parents are in control

A parent or guardian may review their athlete's information, ask us to delete the account and its data, or withdraw permission at any time. The approval email includes a link to delete the account directly, or contact us at the email below.

Contact

Fit Kid Hooper is run by Legends Youth Basketball, a nonprofit.

Questions or requests: info@legendsyba.com. This is the same address our emails come from, so replying to one reaches us too.
