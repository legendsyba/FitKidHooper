Fit Kid Hooper has a new home.

The app is now run by Legends Youth Basketball Association, at a new address. This old version has stopped updating — it takes about a minute to switch over.

Open the new Fit Kid Hooper

How to switch

  1. Tap the button above. That is the new app.
  2. Add it to your home screen.
    iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    Android: tap the menu, then Install app.
  3. Sign in with the same username and PIN you already use.
  4. Delete the old icon. It looks identical, so it is easy to open the wrong one by mistake.

Your progress

Nothing is lost. Workouts, streaks, badges, shot logs and settings are saved to your account, not to this device. Signing in on the new app brings all of it back.

Never made an account?

Then your training lives only in this browser. Save a copy before you switch, and we can load it onto your new account.
